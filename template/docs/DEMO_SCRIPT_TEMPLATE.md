# Demo video script (90 seconds)

One idea carries the video: write it here in one line, then make every shot
serve it. About 200 words of voice-over at a calm pace (2.4 words a second).
Numbers in [brackets] are read off the screen at recording time.

| Time | On screen | Voice-over | Caption |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | Home page, the headline animating in | The problem in one sentence, from the user's side | The question |
| 0:08–0:18 | The thing that makes it different | What it does instead | The promise |
| 0:18–0:40 | Scroll through "How it works", one step per sentence | The three steps, each one sentence | Step words |
| 0:40–0:50 | The hero number | "Here's a result: [figure] means [plain meaning]." | (from the screen) |
| 0:50–1:12 | **The signature moment**: the click, the beads, the stamp, "What just happened" | "Don't take our word for it. One click, and …" | What was proved (sped up, if it was) |
| 1:12–1:21 | The detail page's "what it rests on" | What anyone can check | Every claim, checkable |
| 1:21–1:30 | The core action, or the closing call to action | Who should use it, and the name | URL · built with … |

## Honesty rules

- Anything simulated is labelled on screen while it's shown.
- Anything sped up says so in the caption.
- Never claim in the video what the README's "What's real today" doesn't.

## Recording checklist

- 1440 × 900, 100% zoom, light theme, a fresh browser profile, no extensions.
- Run the signature moment once before recording, so you know it works.
- Move the mouse slowly; cards tilt and the cursor reacts.
- Captions burned in (many judges watch muted). Export 1080p, 30 fps.
- Under 90 seconds; know which shot you'll cut first if it runs long.
